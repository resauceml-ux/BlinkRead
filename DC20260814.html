the eleventh chapter is one of the most distinctive and in some ways most controversial in the entire book, addressing what Hill calls the mystery of sex transmutation and arguing that sexual energy is the most powerful of all human motivating forces and that the conscious redirection of this energy from purely physical expression toward creative and professional achievement is one of the most important and most underappreciated secrets of extraordinary human accomplishment. Hill is careful to note that he is not advocating for abstinence or the suppression of sexual feeling, but for the conscious transmutation, the switching and redirecting of a portion of this powerful energy toward the creative and motivational dimensions of life. Hill grounds this chapter in what he presents as a consistent observation from his research across hundreds of highly successful individuals, namely that the men who achieved the most extraordinary professional and creative results were almost invariably men of exceptional sexual energy, not men who had suppressed or eliminated this energy but men who had learned, whether consciously or through accidental discovery, to channel a significant portion of it into their work and their creative endeavors. the same intensity of feeling, the same capacity for passionate engagement, the same powerful motivational force that drives sexual pursuit, when redirected toward a creative or professional goal, produces a quality of inspired, energized effort that more dispassionate approaches cannot match. the chapter introduces a list of ten mind stimulants that Hill argues can elevate the mind to a higher plane of thought, making available creative and intuitive faculties that ordinary, unstimulated consciousness cannot reliably access. sexual energy appears at the top of this list, followed by love, a burning desire for fame or wealth, music, friendship, a mastermind alliance, mutual suffering, autosuggestion, fear, and narcotics and alcohol, with Hill noting that the last category on the list achieves its effect through destruction of the brain rather than genuine stimulation and therefore represents a false and ultimately counterproductive version of what the others genuinely provide. Hill makes a particularly interesting observation about the age at which most men reach their peak of genuine creative and professional achievement, arguing from his research that this peak typically occurs not in the twenties or early thirties, when physical energy and technical capability are both at their height, but rather in the forties and fifties. his explanation for this seemingly paradoxical finding is that it takes most men that long to develop the emotional maturity, the self mastery, and the practical wisdom to effectively harness and direct the powerful motivational energies that were available to them in earlier years but that were spent rather than invested during those earlier decades. the chapter also addresses the role of love and romance as amplifiers of the creative energy that sexual desire alone initiates. Hill argues that when sexual energy is combined with genuine love and romantic feeling, the resulting emotional compound is the most powerful motivating force available to any human being, capable of producing levels of inspired effort, creative insight, and sustained motivation that neither sexual energy alone nor love alone can generate to the same degree. this combination is what Hill believes underlies the periods of greatest creative output in the lives of many of the most accomplished artists, writers, musicians, and business builders in history. the chapter closes with a reflection on the broader significance of this principle within Hills overall framework, noting that the conscious harnessing and direction of emotional energy, of which sexual transmutation is the most powerful specific example, represents the most advanced and most productive application of all the other principles he has described. desire provides the direction. faith provides the confidence. planning provides the structure. persistence provides the duration. and the transmuted energy of the most powerful human motivating force provides the fuel that drives all of these other principles to their highest possible levels of effectiveness.
